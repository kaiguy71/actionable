# Survival progression, in order. Finish the earliest unfinished stage before starting a later one.
# Pick ONE concrete goal from the earliest unfinished stage and stay on it until it is done.
# Lines starting with # are comments. Format: stage | what to do | why
1 | Get wood: cut down trees until you hold 20 logs (any wood type), staying near where you started | wood builds the house, table, tools and bed
2 | Small house: craft the logs into planks and build the 5x5 starter house next to the trees ("build house") | shelter for the first night; it becomes home
3 | Crafting table inside the house, then craft a wooden door and hang it in the doorway | the house table is where everything is crafted; the door keeps mobs out
4 | Basic tools: wooden sword, then wooden pickaxe (gather more wood first if short) | a weapon before hunting; a pickaxe for stone
5 | Kill sheep: 3 white wool (and mutton for food) from white sheep near home | wool for a bed
6 | Bed: craft a white bed (3 white wool + 3 planks), place it inside the house, sleep when night comes | skips the night, sets spawn
7 | Mine cobblestone near home with the wooden pickaxe (exposed stone, not caves) | stone tools need it
8 | Stone pickaxe and stone sword (then stone axe, shovel); stay within 64 blocks of home until both are made | iron needs a stone pickaxe; a weapon for night
9 | Collect food: kill cows/pigs/chickens or pick sweet berries | health only regenerates when fed
10 | Furnace in the house; cook meat, smelt raw iron | iron and cooked food
11 | Coal only now (furnace and food first, unless raw ore needs smelting): torches from coal + sticks (8-16), mine coal on exposed surface ore | light for caves and for night
12 | Storage room: 2-4 chests (or barrels) next to the house, sort by type | keeps the inventory usable
13 | Iron gear: iron pickaxe, sword, axe, shovel, helmet/chestplate/leggings/boots, shield, bucket | survive and mine diamonds
14 | Reliable food: wheat/carrot/potato farm with water, plus a cow/sheep/chicken pen to breed and slaughter | no more hunting trips
15 | Mob drops: simple passive mob farm (cow ranch) or fenced breeding ranch with a kill chamber | steady leather, meat, wool
16 | Mine at y=-58..-54 for diamonds (cave gear first: torches, food, weapon, iron pickaxe); diamond pickaxe, sword | strongest early tools
17 | Obsidian (diamond pickaxe + water on lava) x10-14, flint and steel | nether portal
18 | Enchanting: bookshelves, enchanting table, enchant tools; anvil | stronger gear
19 | Nether: gold armor or piglin trade, fire resistance if possible, find a fortress, kill blazes for 7+ blaze rods | blaze rods give blaze powder
20 | Gather ender pearls (kill endermen at night or trade with piglins) | eyes of ender
21 | Craft 12+ eyes of ender (ender pearl + blaze powder), cook food, 64+ blocks, bow+arrows, water bucket, bed | stronghold navigation
22 | Throw eyes of ender to find the stronghold, dig down to the portal room, fill the portal frame | reach the End
23 | The End: destroy end crystals on the pillars, then kill the Ender Dragon (beds exploding or sword hits when it perches) | final boss
