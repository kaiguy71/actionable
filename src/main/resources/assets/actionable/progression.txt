# Survival progression. Finish the earliest unfinished stage before starting a later one.
# Pick ONE concrete goal from the earliest unfinished stage and stay on it until it is done.
# Format: stage | what to do | why. Lettered lines (14A, 14B) are OPTIONS of one stage: pick the one that fits
# the terrain and your inventory, finish it, and skip the others unless you want more of that stage.
# Branches D (diamonds) and N (nether) may be done in either order; both come before the End prep.
# Lines starting with # are comments and are not shown to the model.
1 | Get wood: cut down trees until you hold 20 logs (any wood type), staying near where you started | wood builds the house, table, tools and bed
2 | Small house: craft the logs into planks and build the 5x5 starter house next to the trees ("build house") | shelter for the first night; it becomes home
3 | Craft and place a crafting table inside the house, then craft a wooden door and hang it in the doorway | the crafting table is where complex blocks are crafted; the door keeps mobs out
4 | Basic tools: wooden sword, then wooden pickaxe (gather more wood first if short) | a weapon before hunting; a pickaxe for stone
5 | Kill sheep: 3 white wool (and mutton for food) from white sheep near home | wool for a bed
6 | Bed: craft a white bed (3 white wool + 3 planks), place it inside the house, sleep when night comes | skips the night, sets spawn
7 | Mine cobblestone near home with the wooden pickaxe | stone tools need it
8 | Stone pickaxe and stone sword (then stone axe, shovel); until both are made stay within 96 blocks horizontally and 30 vertically of home | iron needs a stone pickaxe; a weapon for night
9 | Collect food: hunt cows/pigs/chickens or pick sweet berries | health only regenerates when fed
10 | Furnace in the house; cook meat, smelt raw iron | iron and cooked food
11 | Coal: mine coal ore, craft 8-16 torches (coal + stick); torches are placed automatically when it is dark | light for caves and for night
12 | Storage room: 2-4 chests (or barrels) next to the house, sort by type (tools, food, building blocks, ores, mob drops) | keeps the inventory usable
13 | Iron gear: iron pickaxe, sword, axe, shovel, helmet/chestplate/leggings/boots, shield, bucket (mine iron ore in caves or the house staircase mine, smelt it) | survive and mine diamonds
14 | Reliable food: build ONE of the options below, then stock 32+ cooked food in the storage room | no more hunting trips
14A | Crop farm: find flat ground next to water (or dig a 1-block trench and fill it with a water bucket); hoe a 9x9 of dirt around the water (water hydrates 4 blocks each way); plant wheat, carrots or potatoes; fence it; harvest when fully grown and replant; craft bread (3 wheat) | renewable vegetarian food
14B | Animal ranch: fence a 10x10 pen near home; lead in 2 cows, 2 sheep, 2 pigs or 2 chickens with wheat/seeds/carrots; breed each pair, keep 2 adults and slaughter the extras; cook the meat | meat, leather, wool on demand
14C | Fishing: craft a fishing rod (3 sticks + 2 string), stand at a water edge and fish; cook the fish | food with no setup, but slow
15 | Mob drops: build ONE of the options below
15A | Simple cow farm: a fenced 5x5 pen with a 1-block-deep pit in a corner so cows can be funnelled to a spot where you hit them; collect drops in a chest | steady leather and beef
15B | Breeding ranch with a killing alcove: a pen with a gated 1x2 alcove; breed animals, herd the extra adults into the alcove and slaughter them for drops | cheap steady meat, wool and leather
15C | Hostile mob trap (advanced, optional): a dark 9x9 platform 24+ blocks above ground with a water channel to a drop chamber, so monsters spawn and fall into a chest | bones, string, gunpowder, XP
16 | Optional home upgrades: enchanting table (4 obsidian, 2 diamonds, 1 book) + 15 bookshelves, anvil, extra rooms, lighting around the house | stronger gear; safer home
D1 | DIAMONDS branch. Prepare for caves: iron pickaxe, sword, shield, 16+ torches, 20+ cooked food, water bucket, 64 cobblestone, bed placed at home | safe deep mining
D2 | Dig a staircase or shaft to y=-58..-45 (stay away from lava; pour water over it), mine 12+ diamond ore, craft the diamond pickaxe and sword | strongest early tools
D3 | Full diamond armor: diamond helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots (24 diamonds for armor alone; mine more if short) | takes most of the damage in the dragon fight
D4 | Optional: enchant the diamond gear and a bow (Protection, Sharpness, Power); repair at an anvil | much stronger gear
N1 | NETHER branch. Obsidian: 10-14 obsidian (diamond pickaxe, water poured on lava sources), plus flint and steel | the portal frame
N2 | Build the portal (4x5 frame, 10 obsidian minimum) and light it. Bring iron gear or better, a shield, food, 64+ cobblestone, a water bucket, a bow, and a fire resistance potion if you have one | safe nether trip
N3 | In the nether: tunnel through cobblestone instead of crossing open ground, find a nether fortress, kill blazes for 7+ blaze rods; wear a gold piece and trade gold ingots with piglins for ender pearls | blaze rods become blaze powder
N4 | Return to the overworld; craft blaze powder from the blaze rods | for eyes of ender
E1 | END PREP (do D and N first, or at least have iron gear, a bow and N finished): gather 12+ ender pearls (endermen at night, or piglin trades) | eyes of ender
E2 | Craft 12+ eyes of ender (ender pearl + blaze powder); bring cooked food, 128+ blocks, bow + 64 arrows, water bucket, sword, spare beds, full armor | stronghold and dragon fight
E3 | Throw eyes of ender to find the stronghold, dig down to the portal room, fill the portal frame, and jump in | reach the End
E4 | The End: destroy end crystals on the pillars (shoot or hit them, mind the caged ones), then defeat the Ender Dragon (exploding beds or sword hits when it perches) | final boss
